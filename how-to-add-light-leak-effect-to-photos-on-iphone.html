How to Add a Light Leak or Lens Flare Effect to Photos on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Velvi developer

Short answer. In Velvi open Effects and choose Light Leak or Flare. Tap the photo where the light should come from and adjust intensity. The flare also draws the rings and streaks a real lens makes along the axis from the light source, so it reads as shot against the sun rather than a sticker. Effects are free.

Step by step

  1. Open your photoTap Edit a photo and pick a picture.
  2. Open EffectsPick Effects in the tool strip.
  3. Choose an effectFlare, Light Leak, Bokeh, Dust, Glow or Chromatic.
  4. Place the lightTap the photo where the sun or light source should be. The tap sets the position.
  5. Tune intensityDrag the slider until the effect fits the scene.
  6. Apply and exportTap the check mark and export. The effect is saved as a setting, so you can edit it again later.

Frequently asked questions

Are the effects made from stock images?

No. They are generated procedurally, so they match your photo's resolution and orientation and do not repeat.

Is it free?

Opening Effects is free; a few individual items inside may be marked Pro.

Can I move the light afterwards?

Yes, because the effect is stored as a setting rather than baked into the picture.

What effects are included?

Flare, Light Leak, Bokeh, Dust, Glow and Chromatic.

Does it work on vertical photos?

Yes. Geometry follows the frame, so the flare does not stretch.

Can I combine it with AI?

Yes. For example, add AI Golden Hour first and a light leak on top.

Does it need internet?

No. Effects run on the device.
