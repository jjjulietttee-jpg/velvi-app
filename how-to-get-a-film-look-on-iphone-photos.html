How to Get a Film Look on iPhone Photos

Updated October 2026 · By the Velvi developer

Short answer. In Velvi open Filters, choose the Film category, pick a look such as Gold 200, Ektar, Superia, Portra or Chrome, then fine-tune Fade, Grain and Warmth. For a stronger, scene-aware result, try the AI Film effect in AI Tune. Film filters are built on color tables with lifted blacks and split-toned shadows rather than simply raising contrast.

Step by step

  1. Open your photoTap Edit a photo and choose a picture.
  2. Open FiltersPick Filters in the tool strip and switch to the Film category.
  3. Choose a film lookTry Gold 200, Ektar, Superia, Portra or Chrome. Previews are drawn on your own photo before you apply.
  4. Fine-tuneUse Adjust filter for Fade, Grain and Warmth.
  5. Optional: add AI Film or dustAI Tune has AI Film, Cinematic, Retro 70s and Pastel Fade. The Effects tool adds Dust for a scanned-negative feel.
  6. ExportTap the check mark and export or share.

Frequently asked questions

Are the film filters free?

Filters are part of the free core. AI Film and the other AI effects need Pro.

How is this different from a basic filter?

Film looks use color tables, with raised blacks and different color in shadows and highlights, which sliders for contrast and saturation cannot reproduce.

Can I add film grain?

Yes. Grain, Fade and Warmth are available for each filter, and Dust adds film dust in Effects.

Can I edit again later?

Yes. Projects keep the original and the edit list, so you can reopen and change the filter.

Is there an AI film option?

Yes, AI Film in AI Tune, along with Cinematic, Retro 70s and Pastel Fade. It takes about 20 seconds in the cloud.

Do film filters work offline?

Yes. Filters, Fade, Grain and Warmth run on the device; only AI effects need internet.

Can I compare with the original?

Yes, with the before/after button or by holding your finger on the photo.
