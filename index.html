Velvi Get the App

AI effects, film looks and face retouch for iPhone photos

Velvi is an iPhone photo editor that combines 23 AI effects (golden hour, blue hour, flash, film, restore and more) with the manual tools people expect: face retouch, film filters, light leaks, brush, background blur and cut-out, collage, text and stickers. Edits are non-destructive: each project keeps the original photo plus the list of changes, so you can reopen it later and move any slider. Crop, filters, Auto, text, effects, brush and basic adjustments are free; AI effects and the rest of the toolset need Velvi Pro.

What Velvi does

  • 23 AI effects in six categoriesPopular, Portrait, Flash, Seasonal, Film and Quality: Golden Hour, Dreamy, Blue Hour, Neon Night, Flash, Y2K Digicam, AI Film, Cinematic, Retro 70s, HD Enhance, Restore Photo, Unblur, Colorize and more. A strength slider blends the result with your original.
  • Face retouch that keeps the skin realSmooth, skin tone, glow, face slimming, eyes, nose and lips, plus makeup and hair color. Smoothing keeps pores instead of blurring the face, and there is a before/after hold on the photo.
  • Film looks and colorA Film filter category (Gold 200, Ektar, Superia, Portra, Chrome and others) with Fade, Grain and Warmth controls, 15 adjustments, one-tap Auto and HSL across 8 color ranges.
  • Lens flare, light leak and bokehThe Effects tool adds Flare, Light Leak, Bokeh, Dust, Glow and Chromatic fringing. You tap the photo to say where the light comes from.
  • Background tools on the devicePortrait blur, black-and-white background, solid color or a clean cut-out, using the iPhone's own subject detection.
  • Brush, collage, text and stickersBrush tools for mosaic, local smoothing, blur, sharpen, dodge, burn, clone and patch; 16 collage layouts; text and stickers on layers you can reorder and edit later.

Frequently asked questions

What is Velvi?

An iPhone photo editor with AI effects (golden hour, film, flash, restore), face retouch, film filters, light leaks, background tools, collage, text and stickers.

Is Velvi free?

The core is free: crop, filters, Auto, text, effects, brush and the basic adjustments. AI effects and the rest of the toolset need Velvi Pro, which is $4.99 per week with a 3-day free trial or $49.99 per year in the US.

Does Velvi send my photos to a server?

AI effects are processed in the cloud through Velvi's own proxy, so the photo you apply an AI effect to is uploaded for that request. Retouch, filters, light effects, brush and background tools run on the device. See the privacy policy for how face data is handled.

Are my edits destructive?

No. A project stores the original photo and the list of changes, so you can reopen it later and change any setting. Nothing is final until you export.

Can I choose the export size and format?

Yes. You choose size (original, 2048, 1440 or 1080 px), format and quality, and share straight from the editor.

What iPhones does it support?

Velvi is an iPhone app and requires iOS 17 or later.
