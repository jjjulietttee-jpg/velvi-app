Frequently asked questions

Does Velvi put a watermark on exports?

No. Its Pro paywall lists full resolution without watermarks, and the free tools do not stamp one.

What does Velvi have that Picsart has?

Collage with 16 layouts, text and stickers on layers, shapes, background blur and cut-out, effects and retouch.

What does Velvi not have?

Velvi is a photo editor, not a video or design suite, and does not have Picsart's large community or template library.

Is Snapseed really free?

Its store listing shows no in-app purchases.

How much is Velvi Pro?

$4.99 per week with a 3-day trial, or $49.99 per year in the US.

Which is cheaper per year?

Velvi Pro at $49.99 is below Picsart Gold at about $57 in US research from October 2026, and Snapseed is free.

Where is the free-start boundary?

Crop, filters, Auto, text, effects, brush and basic adjustments are free; AI effects and other Pro tools need a subscription.
